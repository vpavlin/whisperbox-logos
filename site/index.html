Encrypted forms for Android and Logos Basecamp

Ask in public. Read in private.

WhisperBox is a forms and surveys app for Android and Logos Basecamp (desktop) that works without a server. Share a form by link or QR code; each answer is sealed on the respondent's own device to the form's key, so the network carries only noise and only you can open it. No account to sign up for.

What it does

A full forms app, not a demo. Everything below works on Android and in Basecamp, and a form made on one is answered on the other.

Forms with real question types

Short and long text, single and multiple choice with "Other", dropdown, yes/no, scales (numbers or stars), number, date, time, email, link and section breaks. Templates, preview and optional shuffling.

Share by link or QR code

There is no public directory of forms. People see a form only when you send them its link or show the QR code.

Anonymous forms

Respondents answer under a one-off identity derived for that form. Still one answer per person, with receipts and edits, but you can't link an answer to their address or to their answers on other forms.

Members only

Limit a form to a list of addresses. Respondents sign inside the sealed answer, and unsigned or unlisted answers are dropped. (Can't be combined with anonymous.)

Co-owners

Seal a form's key to a colleague. Co-owners read the answers, export CSV and send receipts and replies; closing, re-opening and editing stay with you.

Edit after publishing

Fix a typo or add a question without breaking the link. Everyone gets the new version, and answers already sent stay attached to their questions.

Close and re-open

Close a form by hand, after a number of answers or on a date, or schedule it to publish later. Answers sent while it was closed never count.

Receipts and answer edits

Send a receipt (or confirm all at once) that only the respondent can recognise. On forms that allow it, respondents can fix their answer for a while (15 minutes by default), until you send the receipt.

Private replies

Reply to one answer. Only that respondent can read it, even on an anonymous form, and the phone shows a notification.

Quiz mode

Mark the right answers and points. The answer key is sealed, so respondents can't read it. See scores and averages, then send each person their score privately.

Results and CSV export

A table of answers, a per-question view and a summary for every question type. Export everything to CSV, including quiz scores.

No Google services

The Android app has no Firebase or Play services. New-answer notifications are posted locally and never contain the answers.

How it works

Every change is an event in one append-only log on one shared topic, synced peer to peer over Logos Messaging. Each device keeps its own copy of the log, so late joiners catch up from whoever is online.

  1. You publish a form

    The title, questions and the form's own public key go out signed with your identity. The form key is derived from your identity, so there is nothing extra to back up. Anyone with the link can load the form; only you can change it.

  2. Respondents seal their answers

    Their app encrypts the whole response, including who sent it, to the form's key before it leaves the device. The sealed answer doesn't even say which form it is for. One response per person.

  3. You open, confirm, export

    Your device decrypts the pool, sends receipts and replies, and exports a CSV. A receipt carries a random id that only the respondent recognises.

Who gets to gossip about what

What's public, what's sealed

Forms are meant to be found by the people you send them to, so their definitions are public. Everything inside a response is sealed.

What protects you

  • Each answer is sealed to the form's key on the respondent's device (secp256k1 ECDH, HKDF-SHA256, ChaCha20-Poly1305). On the wire it is an opaque blob with no respondent identity.
  • Each form has its own key, so a leaked form key exposes one form, not your identity or your other forms.
  • Share links name the form's creator. The app never seals an answer to a form whose creator differs from the link, or to a form id that two people published.
  • Receipts and replies use a random id the respondent chose, so they can't be linked to an address. Answers carry a per-form tag, not a device id, so answers to different forms can't be linked by device.
  • On members-only forms, respondents sign inside the sealed answer and the owner drops unsigned or unlisted answers.

What it doesn't hide

  • Store nodes keep the sealed blobs. Logos Messaging keeps channel history in the fleet's store nodes; that's how late joiners catch up. This is a change from the original WhisperBox. The blobs are useless without the form key, and form definitions were public by design.
  • Timing and network-level correlation (when something was sent, from which IP). Anonymous forms hide you from the form's owner, not from the network.
  • The owner and co-owners see everything in an answer.
  • A co-owner keeps access for good: a shared key can't be taken back.

Install

WhisperBox runs on Android phones and inside Logos Basecamp on the desktop. Both speak the same protocol, so a form made on one is answered on the other. There is no WhisperBox server to sign up for.

Android · arm64

From our F-Droid repository

  1. On your phone, tap the button (or scan the QR code) to add the repository to F-Droid.
  2. Install Loam (recommended) and WhisperBox.
  3. Open Loam once and approve WhisperBox when asked.

Desktop · Logos Basecamp 0.2.x

From the Basecamp package repository

  1. Open Basecamp's package manager and add this package repository:
https://apps.vpavlin.xyz/logos-repo.json
  1. Install WhisperBox (package whisperbox). It pulls in whisperbox_core and its dependencies (delivery_module) automatically.
  2. When updating, update whisperbox and whisperbox_core together.

Supported: Basecamp 0.2.x. Basecamp 0.3 support is coming.

Optional: run your own always-on hub

There is no public WhisperBox hub. If you want a box that is always online to keep the log and re-serve sealed answers, run the same core headless under logoscore and drive it from the shell. A hub that only re-serves can't read any answers. Setup is in hub/README.md.

# publish, share, read
whisperbox create --title "Lunch?" \
  --question "radio:Where??Sushi|Tacos"
whisperbox uri form-…
whisperbox responses form-…

Status and limitations

Latest public builds: Android 0.3.10 · Basecamp whisperbox 0.3.8 + whisperbox_core 0.3.8

  • Early software. The protocol is specified and tested across the Android and desktop implementations, but expect rough edges.
  • Android is arm64 only. Basecamp support is 0.2.x; Basecamp 0.3 support is coming.
  • Keycard support (experimental, Android). A form's key can come from a Status Keycard instead of the phone. It has been tested against the Keycard applet in a simulator; a real-card test is still pending.
  • Bluetooth via Loam is experimental and Android only.
  • Clocks matter. Close dates, answer limits and the edit window are judged by the sender's clock, so a respondent with a wrong clock can land just inside or outside a window.
  • Your device does the housekeeping. Automatic closing and scheduled publishing need your device (or your own hub) to be running; limits and dates are still enforced by every reader.
  • Re-ordering or deleting choice options after answers have come in re-labels those answers. Adding options is safe.
  • No co-owner revocation yet; it would need a new form key.
  • Next: validator hubs that can check answers for one form without your identity key, and Basecamp 0.3.
