How it works

Three messages on one shared topic, synced peer to peer over Logos Messaging. Every device keeps its own copy of the log, so late joiners catch up from whoever is online.

  1. You publish a form

    The title, questions and your public key go out signed with your identity. Anyone with the link can load it; nobody can change it.

  2. Respondents seal their answers

    Their app encrypts the whole response, including who sent it, to your key before it leaves the device. One response per person.

  3. You open, confirm, export

    Your device decrypts the pool, sends each respondent a receipt, and exports a CSV. The receipt carries a random id only the respondent recognises.

Who gets to gossip about what

What's public, what's sealed

Forms are meant to be found, so their definitions are public. Everything about a response is sealed.

ItemOn the networkWho can read it
Form title and questionspublicAnyone on the topic
Who may answerpublicAnyone; restricted forms list the allowed addresses
The answerssealedOnly the form's creator
Who answeredsealedOnly the form's creator
ReceiptpublicAnyone sees it was sent; only the respondent can tell it's theirs

The network's store nodes keep sealed blobs so new devices can catch up. Without the form key they are useless; that's the trade for having no server.

Get it

WhisperBox runs inside Logos Basecamp 0.2, on Android phones, or headless on any Linux box as an always-on hub. They all speak the same protocol, so a form made on one is answered on the others.

In Basecamp

Settings → Package Repositories → add this URL, then install WhisperBox from the package manager. It brings its core and the delivery module with it.

https://raw.githubusercontent.com/jimmy-claw/whisperbox-basecamp/main/logos-repo.json

On Android

Install Loam (the one network node your Logos apps share) and WhisperBox from the Loam F-Droid repository, open Loam once and approve WhisperBox. Scan a form's QR code or tap its link to answer; your key never leaves the phone.

As a headless hub

The same core under logoscore, driven from the shell. Setup is in hub/README.md.

# publish, share, read
whisperbox create --title "Lunch?" \
  --question "radio:Where??Sushi|Tacos"
whisperbox uri form-…
whisperbox responses form-…
